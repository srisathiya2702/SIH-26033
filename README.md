## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. The proposed solution is a digital platform that connects farmers directly with customers and bulk buyers. Farmers can upload their products, available quantity, and selling price.

2. The platform helps farmers sell their products without depending on multiple intermediaries. It allows buyers to purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers.

3. The unique feature of this solution is an AI-based smart matching system that connects farmers with suitable buyers based on product availability, demand, and location.
   
## Technical Approach
1. The system will be developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Django, and MySQL. AI and machine learning techniques will be used for demand prediction and delivery planning.

2. The implementation process starts with farmer registration and product listing. Buyers can search for products and place orders. The system then matches buyers with farmers, predicts demand, plans delivery routes, and updates the order status.
   
## Feasibility and Viability
1. The solution is feasible because it uses commonly available software technologies and can be developed as a simple web application. It can initially be tested with a small group of farmers.

2. Possible challenges include poor internet connectivity, lack of digital knowledge, transportation difficulties, and changes in agricultural product availability.

3. These challenges can be managed by providing a simple interface, regional language support, farmer training, local transport partnerships, and regular updates of product information.

## Impact and Benefits
1. The solution benefits farmers, consumers, FPOs, retailers, and wholesalers by improving access to agricultural products and creating better selling opportunities.

2. The platform increases farmers' income, reduces consumer prices, minimizes food wastage, supports rural development, and improves the efficiency of agricultural transportation.
